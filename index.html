The NS Artisan Crest

A Mark of Authentic Woodworking Heritage

Inspired by traditional master carpenters' joinery and the hand-painted storefront signage of Rajanpur, our hand-drafted emblem combines an octagonal wood-beveled shield, an architectural arch, and hand-brushed “NS” — Nazeer Son's — calligraphy in walnut, brass and crimson.

Every joint, polish coat and upholstery stitch is inspected under our family's supervision. The crest is our personal guarantee on every piece that leaves the showroom.
